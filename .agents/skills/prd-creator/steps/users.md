# Users

Lock who the report is for before deciding which pages it has. Every page in the PRD is later grouped under one of these users, so this list is the spine of the in-scope section and of the client's first page.

Propose **2–5 users** (personas), seeded from `_project_/0-intake/handoff.md`'s "Users & stakeholders" section and from the discovery transcripts. A user is a group with its own decisions to make and its own questions for the data, not a job title. Two job titles that ask the same questions are one user. Executives who glance at the headline numbers and analysts who slice them all day are usually two.

For each user, propose:

1. **Name** in the client's vocabulary, as they say it in the transcripts ("Regional sales managers", "The finance team"), not a marketing persona name. This string is reused **verbatim** as a heading in the in-scope section, so settle the exact wording now.
2. **Role tag**: two to four words for what they do with the report ("Tracks the pipeline", "Closes the month").
3. **Who they are**: one to three sentences covering who they are, roughly how many there are if known, and how often they open the report and what they do with what they find.
4. **What they want**: three to five goals in their own terms, the questions they want answered and the decisions they make with the answers, not visuals. "See which regions are behind plan before the Monday call" is a goal; "A variance bar chart" is a visual.

Present them in chat, then use AskUserQuestion:
- Looks right, lock it in
- Mostly right, I'll edit in chat
- Missing a group, let me describe

Lock the list, in the order the users should appear in the PRD (usually by how much of the report is for them), before moving on.
